HUONG DAN THU V0.18.5.3 / PWA V0.22.5.3 FIX3

A. KIEM TRA VI TRI CHU
1. Build bang build_release.cmd.
2. Mo truc tiep file DWG goc So do ket day TQ-HG (1)(1).dwg, KHONG mo project da save bang FIX1/FIX2 de danh gia lan dau.
3. Zoom vao cac nhanh co nhan 2 dong va chu sat duong day/thiet bi.
4. So sanh cac nhan nhu LBS 374/04 - Xuan Minh, 373-7/01 - T. Son Thanh, TD Nam Khoa, Tan Quang...
5. Kiem tra dong 1, dong 2, khoang cach dong va khoang ho so voi duong day/thiet bi.
6. Save Project, dong tab, mo lai Project va kiem tra vi tri khong thay doi.

B. QUET/CHUYEN BANG MA TIENG VIET - C#
1. Mo khu vuc Tim/Thay the chu -> Chuan hoa bang ma tieng Viet.
2. De AUTO, nguong 80%, bam Quet + xem truoc.
3. Xem thong ke Unicode/TCVN3/VNI/VIQR va toi da 6 mau truoc -> sau.
4. Neu style/font cu bi khai bao sai, co the ep TCVN3, VNI-Windows hoac VIQR roi Quet + xem truoc lai.
5. Bam Chuyen chu dang chon hoac Chuyen tat ca -> Unicode.
6. Neu ket qua khong dung, Ctrl+Z de hoan tac.

C. PWA
- Cong cu tuong duong nam trong khu Tim/Thay the chu.
- Co AUTO/TCVN3/VNI/VIQR/Unicode, nguong 70/80/90%, xem truoc, chuyen selected/all va Undo.

D. NGUYEN TAC AN TOAN
- AUTO 80% la mac dinh khuyen nghi.
- Luon Quet + xem truoc truoc khi Chuyen tat ca doi voi ban ve cu khong ro font/bang ma.
- Chu Unicode dang dung nhu TUYEN QUANG, CHIEM HOA, Tan Quang khong duoc phep bi map lai trong AUTO.
