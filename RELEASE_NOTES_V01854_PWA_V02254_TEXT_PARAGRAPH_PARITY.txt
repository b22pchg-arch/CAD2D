NGUYEN NHAN BO SUNG XAC DINH TU DWG MAU
1. CAD Height khong tuong duong truc tiep voi font em-size cua Skia/Canvas.
   - FIX3 van dat paint.TextSize = CAD Height.
   - Voi font Arial/Arial substitute, chieu cao cap glyph "H" chi chiem mot phan em-box.
   - FIX4 do cap-height cua "H" va tang em-size sao cho cap-height hien thi = CAD Height.
2. Nhieu MTEXT hai dong trong DWG co paragraph format \pxqc; (q=c, Center).
   - Parser cu bo toan bo \p...; truoc khi luu model nen mat can giua doan van.
   - Sau khi mat q=c, hai dong bi ve tu mep trai cua reference rectangle, tao cam giac sai vi tri ro ret.
3. MTEXT LineSpacingStyle=AtLeast can giu baseline dau on dinh.
   - FIX3 chia residual ActualHeight len tren va xuong duoi, co the lam ca block chu dich theo Y.
   - FIX4 giu first baseline theo cap-height/glyph top; phan chenh ActualHeight duoc dua vao baseline gap khi AtLeast.
