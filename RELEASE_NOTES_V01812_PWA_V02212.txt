DWG SKETCH PROTOTYPE V0.18.12.0 / PWA V0.22.12
ROLE-DERIVED OUTPUTS + CHAINED RESULT ROLES
============================================================

MUC TIEU
- Cho phep macro tao ra mot tap doi tuong ket qua va dung ngay tap do cho cac buoc sau.
- Bo sung runtime role Result va OutputRole cho tung macro step.
- Giam nhu cau chon/giao lai doi tuong thu cong giua cac buoc cua mot macro CAD phuc hop.

THAY DOI CHINH
1. Macro step co them OutputRole: None / Result / Target.
2. RCOPY moi mac dinh OutputRole=Result.
3. Cac relative step RMOVE/RCOPY/RROTATE/RSCALE/RMIRROR co the publish tap chon sau khi thanh cong vao Result hoac Target.
4. Step sau co the dung InputRole=Result va/hoac ReferenceRole=Result.
5. Result la runtime-derived role: khong cho gan thu cong; tu dong xoa truoc moi MACROPLAY de khong dung nham ket qua cu.
6. MACROCHECK kiem tra chuoi theo thu tu. Neu Result duoc dung truoc producer, preflight FAIL va khong thay doi ban ve.
7. OutputRole=Target cho phep chuoi tao lai Target runtime cho cac buoc sau.
8. Sau playback, selection ban dau cua nguoi dung van duoc khoi phuc; Result van duoc giu de kiem tra bang MACRORESULT.
9. Lenh moi: MACRORESULT / MACROSELECTRESULT / MACRORESULTCLEAR / MACROCLEARRESULT.
10. Macro JSON tang len schemaVersion 6 / engineVersion 0.18.12.0. Macro cu khong co OutputRole van tuong thich.

TUONG THICH VA ON DINH
- Project JSON van version 11.
- Khong luu entity ID cua Source/Target/Reference/Result vao macro JSON.
- FIX4 TEXT/MTEXT, cap-height/em, paragraph alignment, 3-on-5 line spacing va Vietnamese Unicode Normalizer khong thay doi.
- TX3/TX4/TX5, SM1, GE1 va Symbol Update Manager duoc giu nguyen.

CHUOI THU KHUYEN NGHI
