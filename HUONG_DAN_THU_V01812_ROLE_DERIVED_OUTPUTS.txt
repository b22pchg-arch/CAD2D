HUONG DAN THU V0.18.12 - ROLE-DERIVED OUTPUTS + CHAINED RESULT ROLES
===================================================================

A. KHAI NIEM MOI
- Source / Target / Reference: runtime role co the gan tu tap chon nhu V0.18.11.
- Result: runtime role DAN XUAT. Nguoi dung khong gan Result bang MACROBIND; Result chi duoc tao boi OutputRole cua mot relative step.
- OutputRole:
  * None   : khong publish tap chon sau step.
  * Result : tap chon sau step tro thanh Result.
  * Target : tap chon sau step tro thanh Target runtime.

B. BAI THU NHANH - RCOPY -> RESULT -> RROTATE
1. Mo ban ve va chon 2-5 doi tuong ro rang.
2. Gan Source bang nut role hoac lenh MACROSOURCE.
3. Tao/ghi macro co it nhat hai step:
   Step 1: RCOPY, InputRole=Source, OutputRole=Result, deltaX/deltaY khac 0.
   Step 2: RROTATE, InputRole=Result, ReferenceRole=SameAsInput (hoac Reference), OutputRole=Result.
4. Chay MACROCHECK. Mong doi PASS.
5. Chay MACROPLAY.
6. Mong doi:
   - Step 1 tao ban sao.
   - Step 2 CHI quay cac ban sao vua tao.
   - Doi tuong Source goc khong bi quay.
7. Chay MACRORESULT. Mong doi tap ban sao sau bien doi duoc chon.
8. Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y de kiem tra history.

C. BAI THU CHUOI RESULT -> TARGET
1. Them Step 3: RMOVE, InputRole=Result, OutputRole=Target.
2. MACROCHECK -> PASS.
3. MACROPLAY.
4. Sau playback, Target runtime phai tro vao tap ket qua cua Step 3.
5. Mot step thu 4 co InputRole=Target phai dung duoc tap vua publish ma khong can MACROTARGET lai.

D. BAI THU PREFLIGHT BAT BUOC
1. Dat Step 1: RROTATE, InputRole=Result.
2. Khong co step producer Result o truoc.
3. MACROCHECK phai FAIL voi y nghia: Result duoc dung truoc khi duoc tao.
4. MACROPLAY cung phai bi chan truoc khi thay doi ban ve.
5. Chuyen RCOPY(out=Result) len truoc RROTATE. MACROCHECK phai PASS neu cac dieu kien khac hop le.

E. BAI THU RESULT STALE
1. Phat macro thanh cong de tao Result.
2. MACRORESULT phai chon duoc Result.
3. Thay doi Source va phat lai macro.
4. Result cu phai bi xoa ngay khi bat dau playback; ket qua sau cung chi la Result cua lan phat moi.
5. MACRORESULTCLEAR phai xoa Result runtime.

F. BAI THU REFERENCE=RESULT
1. Step 1: RCOPY Source -> Result.
2. Step 2: RSCALE InputRole=Source, ReferenceRole=Result, OutputRole=None.
3. MACROCHECK phai PASS vi Result da co producer o Step 1.
4. Step 2 phai dung bounds/anchor cua Result lam reference geometry, nhung bien doi Source.

G. BAI THU TUONG THICH MACRO CU
1. Import/mo macro V0.18.11 hoac cu hon.
2. OutputRole trong cac step cu duoc coi la None.
3. CurrentSelection / Source / Target / Reference va adaptive anchors phai hoat dong nhu ban cu.
4. Project JSON van version 11.

H. DIEU KIEN DAT
- MACROCHECK chan Result-before-producer.
- RCOPY publish dung cac ban sao, khong publish Source goc.
- Result co the duoc chain qua nhieu relative step.
- OutputRole=Target co the tao Target runtime moi.
- Selection truoc playback duoc khoi phuc sau playback.
- Result khong duoc serialize thanh entity IDs trong macro file.
