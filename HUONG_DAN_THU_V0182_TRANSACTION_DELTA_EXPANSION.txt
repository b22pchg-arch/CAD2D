DWG SKETCH V0.18.2 / PWA V0.22.2
HUONG DAN THU - TRANSACTION DELTA EXPANSION
===========================================

A. C# / WPF
1. Mo lai DWG lon da chay on dinh o V0.18.1.
2. Chay ARCHCHECK.
   - Mong doi: Health=OK.
3. Chay TXINFO truoc khi thu de ghi nhan Snapshot/Delta ban dau.
4. Thu SCALE:
   - Chon 1 va nhieu doi tuong, SCALE theo base point.
   - Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y phai phuc hoi dung hinh hoc, layer, mau, text va metadata.
5. Thu ROTATE:
   - Thu +/-15 do va ROTATE theo base point.
   - TXINFO phai co ROTATE.
6. Thu MIRROR:
   - Chon nhieu loai LINE/TEXT/POLYLINE/CIRCLE/ARC/DIMENSION.
   - Ve truc hai diem, sau do Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y.
   - TXINFO phai co MIRROR va Snapshot khong tang do rieng lenh MIRROR.
7. Thu PROPERTIES:
   - Sua toa do, text, kich thuoc, goc, mau/net tren doi tuong ho tro.
   - Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y phai phuc hoi dung.
   - TXINFO phai co PROPERTIES va Snapshot khong tang do rieng Apply Properties.
8. Thu MOVE/GRIP:
   - Van hoat dong nhu ban truoc; TXINFO tiep tuc co MOVE/GRIP.
9. Thu fallback:
   - COPY/PASTE/DELETE/TRIM/EXTEND van Undo/Redo duoc bang snapshot legacy.
10. Thu DWG lon:
   - Pan/zoom, select, OSNAP, grip, SCALE/ROTATE/MIRROR lien tuc.
   - Khong duoc xuat hien nhap nhay hoac regression render.
