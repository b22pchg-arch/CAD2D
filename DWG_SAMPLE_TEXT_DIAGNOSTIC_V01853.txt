Observation:
- Labels with the same nominal H can have different ActualHeight because glyphs/diacritics differ.
- Therefore a single global Y shift or a fixed first-baseline factor cannot reproduce the DWG consistently.
- FIX3 uses ActualHeight as the absolute block extent and splits the residual around the first/last baseline using the rendered glyph ascent/descent ratio.
