HUONG DAN THU V0.18.9.0 / PWA V0.22.9

A. RROTATE
1. Chon mot nhom A.
2. Bat dau ghi macro.
3. ROTATE nhom A quanh mot diem goc bat ky, vi du +30 do.
4. Dung va luu macro.
5. Trong Step Editor phai thay RROTATE, referenceMode=SelectionBoundsCenter,
   pivotOffsetX/pivotOffsetY va angleDeg.
6. Chon nhom B o vi tri khac va phat macro.
7. Nhom B phai quay +30 do quanh pivot co cung do lech so voi tam bounds cua B.
8. Ctrl+Z/Redo phai hoan tac/phuc hoi dung mot transform.

B. RSCALE
1. Ghi SCALE x1.5 quanh mot diem khong trung tam tap chon.
2. Phat tren tap chon khac.
3. Kiem tra pivot duoc tai tao tu SelectionBoundsCenter + offset va scale dung x1.5.

C. RMIRROR
1. Ghi MIRROR theo mot truc hai diem.
2. Step Editor phai co 4 offset cua A/B so voi SelectionBoundsCenter.
3. Phat tren nhom khac o vi tri khac.
4. Truc phai di theo nhom moi, khong quay ve toa do ban ve luc ghi.

D. PARAMETER PROFILE
- Thu sua ROTATE1_ANGLE, SCALE1_FACTOR hoac MIRROR1_AX/AY/BX/BY trong profile.
- Phat lai va kiem tra hinh hoc thay doi theo profile.
